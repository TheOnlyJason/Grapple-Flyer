## Description (4000 chars max)

> GALE is a one-touch sky-sailing runner about momentum.
>
> Hold to fire your tether at a floating anchor and swing. Release at just
> the right moment to slingshot forward — nail the angle and a PERFECT
> release rockets you across the sky. Skim the cloud sea to charge your wind
> gauge, then unleash a dash that smashes straight through debris.
>
> The world drifts from dawn to dusk to starry night as you fly: snowy
> peaks, pine forests, ancient ruins, monuments, and floating islands with
> their own tiny wildlife. Every run is procedurally scattered — no two
> routes are the same.
>
> FEATURES
> • One-touch play: hold to swing, release to slingshot
> • PERFECT releases, cloud skimming, and wind dashes to master
> • Hit top speed and smash straight through meteors — they shatter
> • Power-ups: a Magnet that pulls everything in, a Barrier that saves you once
> • Golden orbs to collect on every run
> • A living day/night cycle — dawn, golden hour, dusk, and starlit night
> • Ever-changing biomes: forests, peaks, ruins, monuments, floating isles
> • Three pilots to unlock your style: Paper Plane, Monkey, and Rainbow Cat
> • Run objectives to cross off and personal bests to beat
> • No ads. No accounts. No data collection. Just flight.
>
> Easy to pick up, hard to put down — one more run is always calling.

## App Review notes — 1.2

> GALE 1.2 is a content update to the approved 1.1 (same app, same developer).
>
> New in 1.2: two power-up pickups (Magnet and Barrier), a collectible called orbs, a shatter animation when a meteor is smashed, and objectives that are crossed off when completed.
>
> About orbs: they are an in-game collectible, counted on screen and saved on the device. They are earned only by playing. They cannot be bought and there is nothing to spend them on in this version. The app has NO in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and makes no network calls at runtime.
>
> Otherwise unchanged: a single-player, fully offline arcade game. No login or setup is needed. Launch, tap the play button, hold anywhere to fire the tether and swing, release to slingshot, tap DASH when the wind gauge is full. The arrows on the menu change the pilot.
>
> To see the new items, fly for 15 to 25 seconds: gold rings are orbs (fly through them, the counter is at the top left); a pink badge is the Magnet and a violet badge is the Barrier (fly through one to pick it up). To see a meteor shatter: a PERFECT release usually reaches smash speed. The pilot then gains a mint ring and the speed label at the bottom left reads SPEED · SMASH for about a second; flying into a meteor while that shows shatters it. A wind dash (the DASH button, once the gauge is full) also shatters meteors in its path.
>
> Rated 4+. English-only and identical in all regions.
